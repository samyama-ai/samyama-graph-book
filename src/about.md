# About the Project & Author

## Samyama.ai: The Vision
Samyama Graph is sponsored and developed by [Samyama.ai](https://samyama.ai), a company dedicated to building the future of autonomous, hardware-accelerated knowledge systems. Our mission is to unify the fragmented data landscape of graphs, vectors, and optimization into a single "Mechanical Sympathy" engine.

For enterprise inquiries, partnerships, or support, visit our official website: [https://samyama.ai](https://samyama.ai)
